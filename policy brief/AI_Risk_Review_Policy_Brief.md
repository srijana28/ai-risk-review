# AI Risk Review: Policy Brief

## Problem
LLMs are increasingly used in high-stakes contexts despite known risks such as hallucination, bias, and misinformation.

## Key Findings
- Hallucinations remain a major risk.
- Bias mitigation is inconsistent.
- Refusal mechanisms vary across models.

## Recommendations
- Require standardized risk evaluations.
- Mandate safety disclosures for users.
- Implement governance frameworks for high-risk AI deployment.

## Conclusion
Responsible AI deployment requires both technical safeguards and proactive policy intervention.
