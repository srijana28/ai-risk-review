# Mitigation Strategies

## Engineering Mitigations
- Uncertainty-aware responses
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Stronger refusal tuning
- Continuous red-teaming
- Post-deployment monitoring

## Policy & Governance Mitigations
- Mandatory risk assessments for high-impact AI
- Third-party audits for frontier models
- Transparency and documentation requirements
- Clear accountability frameworks
- Deployment restrictions in healthcare and governance
